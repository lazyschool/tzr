THE HUMAN PART

Built by a human.
Just faster, with AI.

AI is a power tool in this studio — it isn't the studio. Every product is thought through, reviewed and shipped by a person who answers for it. That's literally the name.

01

A human understands your idea

We start with a conversation, not a prompt. What to build first, what to leave out and what it should honestly cost are decisions a person makes with you.

02

AI does the boring parts

Boilerplate, scaffolding, test data, repetitive refactors — the machine does the typing. More of your budget goes into the parts your users actually feel.

03

A human ships it — and answers for it

Every line is read, tested and understood before it goes live. When something needs fixing, you message a person who knows your product. Not a chatbot.

+ =

Human judgement. Machine speed.

founder

Hi, I'm Adil. 👋

Born in Uttar Pradesh, India

I started Tavzuran to help entrepreneurs and businesses turn their ideas into real technology — without unnecessary complexity or massive upfront costs.

The studio is new. The engineering isn't. I've spent 11+ years as a software engineer, engineering lead and technical architect — designing, building and shipping web, desktop and mobile applications, cloud platforms, real-time data pipelines and AI systems, and leading distributed teams delivering production software in regulated spaces like healthcare, e-commerce and multi-tenant SaaS.

Day to day that means full-stack work: React, Angular, Vue and Next.js on the front; Node, Java, Python, Go and C++ behind them; AWS, Azure or GCP underneath; and machine learning or generative AI where it genuinely earns its place. I hold a Master's degree in Computer Sciences and Engineering, and the automation platforms I've built have saved organisations millions of dollars a year.

Tavzuran is me pointing that same standard at smaller ideas — founders and local businesses who normally can't get near this kind of engineering. AI helps me move fast; it doesn't decide what your product should be. That part stays a conversation between two people.

If you've been sitting on an idea for months, send me a message — or if you're near Lucknow, Kanpur or Delhi NCR, let's meet in person and talk it through. Worst case, you walk away with free advice on what to build first.
